## What's new

- **Keeps every drive until you're home.** Glovebox now keeps each minute of driving on its own, skips the time the car sits parked, and holds it until it's backed up. A day parked at work on Sentry no longer pushes the morning drive out.
- **Settings → Storage.** A bar shows what's on the card: the car's recordings, Sentry and saved clips, kept drives and free space. A slider picks how many hours of driving to keep, measured from your own car.
- **Better default split.** New cards give the car half the space, 32 to 64 GB, and keep the rest for drives.
- **Snapshots every 15 minutes** instead of every hour.
- **Cleaner web app.** Shorter text, details behind **?** tips, a compact Car page once paired.
- **Smoother playback** from one minute to the next: no black frame, and the speed and gear don't reset.

## Fixes

- Drives were lost when space ran low: the oldest snapshots went first, even when they held driving not yet backed up.
- Backups failed with "no such clip file" when a snapshot was cleared mid-upload.
- Time parked in the garage showed up as a drive, and made the drive home look longer.
- The accelerator pedal read 100 times too high.
- After a restart, Settings said it was waiting for a backup that had finished.

## Upgrading

Your car's drive keeps its size. To keep more driving, use **Settings → Storage**: it recreates the car's drive at the new size, once everything is backed up.
